Why Harbor

London doesn’t rent by the flat. It rents by the room. Most property software assumes one tenant per unit. Harbor is built rooms-first, so your numbers match how you actually let.

16rooms, each with its own tenancy16 rooms, each with its own tenancy

It isn’t about properties. It’s about rooms.

Every room has its own price, status, tenant and tenancy. Occupancy, rent roll, vacancy loss, arrears and profit all add up from the rooms.

One system.
Enquiry to deposit return.

What’s built in:

  1. 01

    Every room tracked on its own. Price, status, availability date, tenant and tenancy, room by room.

  2. 02

    One system, start to finish. Applications, bookings, contracts, rent, deposits, notice and move-out. Nothing re-keyed.

  3. 03

    Deposit protection built in. Register and protect deposits, download prescribed information and handle repayments in Harbor.

  4. 04

    Certificates that chase their own renewals. Eight certificate types per property. Red when expired, amber 30 days before.

  5. 05

    An AI assistant that knows your portfolio. Ask it anything, like who’s late with rent this month, and get the answer from your own data. It also triages repairs and works out an acquisition’s break-even.

What it does

How Harbor runs your rooms.

  1. 1

    Enquiries from Zoopla and other property portals arrive in one inbox, ready to work.

    Leads

  2. 2

    Each room has its own price, status, availability date, tenant and tenancy. Portfolio figures add up from the rooms.

    Rooms

  3. 3

    Upload a statement from Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest or Santander, or any CSV or PDF. Payments are matched to expected rent and mismatches are flagged.

    Rent

  4. 4

    Register and protect deposits with the approved schemes, download prescribed information and handle repayments without leaving Harbor.

    Deposits

  5. 5

    Gas Safety, EICR, EPC, HMO licence and four more, per property. Red when expired, amber 30 days before.

    Compliance

  6. 6

    Run every portfolio from one place, with monthly profit and loss for each, including what empty rooms cost you between lets.

    Profit

Every room, every payment, every certificate. Backed by a 24/7 help desk.
