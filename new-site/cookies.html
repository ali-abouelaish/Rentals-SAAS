1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser, for example to keep you signed in.

2. This website

This website doesn’t set cookies, and it doesn’t use tracking or advertising tools. Fonts and some code are loaded from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when your browser fetches them.

3. Signing in to Harbor Ops

When you sign in, Harbor Ops uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your account. The service can’t work without them, so they don’t need your consent. [List of cookies and how long they last to confirm].

4. Managing cookies
